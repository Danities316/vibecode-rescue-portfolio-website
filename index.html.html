FAQ

Questions founders ask us.

Can you fix applications built with Lovable?

Yes. Lovable apps are a core focus. We review the code and setup, find the cause of the problem and fix the agreed issues.

Do you work with Bolt.new and Replit?

Yes. We work with apps from Bolt.new, Replit and other AI coding tools.

Can you fix an application you didn’t build?

Yes. Most of our work is on apps built by someone or something else. We start with a review so we understand the codebase before changing it.

What if I don’t understand the technical problem?

That’s fine. Tell us what you see happening, and we’ll diagnose it and explain it in plain language.

Do you work with Nigerian and international customers?

Yes. We support founders in Nigeria and abroad, with prices shown in Naira and indicative USD.

How much does app rescue cost?

Vibe Code Rescue starts from ₦75,000 (indicative $100+). The final quote depends on complexity and scope, and you’ll see it before we begin.

How long does a typical repair take?

It depends on the problem. Once we’ve reviewed your app, your quote will include a realistic timeline.

Can you integrate Paystack?

Yes. We can set up and fix Paystack payments, including checkout flows and webhooks.

Do you offer monthly maintenance?

Yes. The Monthly Technical Partner plan starts from ₦100,000/month (indicative $200+) and covers agreed fixes, small improvements, deployment support and monthly reports.

Is my source code and business information kept confidential?

We treat your code and business information as confidential, and we’re happy to discuss any confidentiality terms you need before we start.
