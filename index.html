What is a Vibe Code Health Check?

An independent human technical assessment of your AI-built application. Within an agreed scope, I review implementation, identify observed issues and risks, classify findings by severity and provide practical next steps in plain language.

My application works. Why do I need a technical review?

A demo shows how an application behaves in the situations you try. It may not reveal insecure permissions, fragile data handling, missing error handling or deployment risks. A review helps you understand these areas before you rely on the app for your business.

Which AI coding platforms do you support?

Lovable, Bolt, Replit and other AI coding platforms. The review focuses on the underlying code, database and integrations. Share your app and stack so I can confirm a suitable scope.

Do I need to share my source code?

A preview link is enough to start the conversation. Reviewing code quality, authentication, database policies and deployment configuration usually requires access to the relevant source code and settings. We agree on limited, appropriate access and any confidentiality needs before work begins. Do not send passwords or secret keys through the enquiry form.

Can you fix problems discovered during the review?

Yes. I can provide a separate scope and quote for fixes or improvements. There is no obligation to buy repair services: you can implement the recommendations yourself or use another developer. The Health Check fee is credited toward Vibe Code Rescue.

Do you work with applications built using Lovable, Bolt and Replit?

Yes. Whether your app works well, has a specific problem or needs more features, I can review the relevant implementation and agree on the help it needs. VibeCode Rescue is independent of these platforms.

How long does a technical review take?

Timing depends on application size, complexity, access and review scope. We agree on a delivery timeline before the review begins. I aim to reply to enquiries within 1 hour and take on a maximum of one active project at a time.

Do you guarantee that my application will be bug-free?

No. A review identifies observed issues and risks within the agreed scope. It cannot guarantee that every weakness will be found, or that your application is completely secure, bug-free or production-ready. Delivered fixes carry a 30-day warranty.

Can I receive technical advice without hiring you for development?

Yes. Select general technical advice in the enquiry form or message me on WhatsApp. We can discuss the question and agree on any paid consultation scope before starting. You do not have to commission development work.
